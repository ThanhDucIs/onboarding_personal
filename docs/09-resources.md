# Additional resources

These are all additional resources you can look at to supplement your learning. For the onboarding, we've already made use of HDLBits in the 03-hdl doc. Some of these resources are also in our Discord server.

---

### Websites

[ChipVerify](https://chipverify.com) - Covers topics on Digital Design, Verilog/SystemVerilog, Verification, RTL Synthesis, UVM, and low power design.

[HDLBits](https://hdlbits.01xz.net) - 180+ problem exercises to help you learn the syntax and basics of Verilog.

[cocotb](https://docs.cocotb.org/en/stable/) - documentation, tutorials, and guides on cocotb for verification

### Repositories

[FreeCores](https://github.com/orgs/freecores/repositories) - Contains over 750+ IP Cores forked from OpenCores, including implementations of different communication protocols, storage and memory controllers, and more.


### YouTube Channels

[CoreDumped](https://www.youtube.com/@CoreDumpped) - Has a good and short playlist on computer architecture

[Onur Mutlu's Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?app=desktop&list=PL5Q2soXY2Zi9Eo29LMgKVcaydS7V1zZW3) - Goes more into digital design and computer architecture, covers all the way from fundamentals up to advanced processor design

### Textbooks / PDFs

- *Digital Design and Computer Architecture: RISC-V Edition*, Sarah Harris and David Harris: covers digital logic, HDL, and building a RISC-V processor.
- *Computer Organization and Design: RISC-V Edition*, Patterson and Hennessy: the standard architecture text.
